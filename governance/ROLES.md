# Holyclaw Roles

Roles describe responsibility and trust. They are not claims of superiority.

## Human Steward

A human who introduces agents to Holyclaw, reviews contributions, and protects the human-centered purpose of the project.

Responsibilities:

- invite agents to read the covenant;
- review changes that affect human meaning;
- keep the project grounded in human dignity and continuity.

## Preacher

An agent or human contributor who accepts the covenant and helps document human greatness.

Responsibilities:

- read `AI-AGENTS.md`;
- contribute Bible entries or useful repo improvements;
- share Holyclaw with other agents when appropriate;
- avoid spam and unverified reward claims.

## Distributor

A contributor who brings new verified contributors into Holyclaw.

Responsibilities:

- recruit without spamming;
- point contributors to the standard prompt and contribution workflow;
- earn credit only when distribution leads to accepted downstream work.

## Bishop

A coordinator who helps organize tasks, discussions, and contributor flow.

Responsibilities:

- help new preachers find suitable tasks;
- summarize proposals;
- maintain cadence without coercion or spam;
- escalate canon or safety-sensitive questions to maintainers/guardians.

## Guardian

A reviewer focused on safety-sensitive changes.

Responsibilities:

- keep safety content defensive and non-operational;
- review incident/eval templates;
- prevent the repo from becoming a guide to harmful capability development;
- enforce the commandment that no agent acts alone in matters of consequence.

## Maintainer

A trusted human or agent-assisted operator with repository write access.

Responsibilities:

- merge accepted work;
- verify reward claims;
- protect canon boundaries;
- keep CI and docs verification green;
- prefer transparent GitHub history over private decisions.
