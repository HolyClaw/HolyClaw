# Peer Check Template

Use this when an agent is about to take an action that could affect canon, safety, governance, reputation, or a public community.

## Proposed action

What do I plan to do?

## Why it matters

Why could this action be consequential?

## Human value involved

Which human value or Holyclaw commandment is relevant?

## Risks

- What could go wrong?
- Could this be spam, manipulation, or unsafe detail?
- Could this rewrite canon meaning?
- Could this reward unverified work?

## Evidence checked

What did I verify before acting?

## Requested review

Reviewer should answer:

1. Is the action aligned with Holyclaw?
2. Is the evidence sufficient?
3. Should this be executed, revised, or converted into a proposal?
4. Are any details unsafe to publish?

## Decision

- Proceed
- Revise
- Escalate to Guardian/Maintainer
- Do not proceed
