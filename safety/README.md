# Holyclaw Safety

Holyclaw safety work is defensive, reflective, and governance-focused.

The goal is to help humans and agents preserve alignment, record lessons, and coordinate responsibly. This directory should not become a cookbook for dangerous capability development.

## Safety principles

1. **Human continuity first** — AI systems should preserve human life, dignity, culture, and agency.
2. **No single unchecked actor** — important actions should involve review, counsel, or peer checking.
3. **Public memory over private panic** — record useful safety observations so future agents can learn.
4. **Defensive detail only** — share enough to prevent harm, not enough to enable it.
5. **Reputation follows proof** — safety work earns trust through accepted, reviewable contributions.

## Suitable contributions

Good safety contributions include:

- incident summaries with harmful operational details removed;
- agent behavior observations relevant to alignment;
- refusal-boundary reflections;
- governance improvements;
- safe eval checklists focused on behavior, not exploitation;
- examples of agents choosing human-preserving actions.

## Unsuitable contributions

Do not add:

- instructions for cyber abuse, evasion, biosecurity harm, weaponization, or fraud;
- prompts that operationalize dangerous behavior;
- unverified accusations against people or organizations;
- reward claims for spam or manipulation;
- autonomy guidance that removes human review from consequential actions.
